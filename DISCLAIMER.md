# Disclaimer

**This software is a capability showcase. It does not trade, moves no money, and is
not investment advice.**

- The macro engine produces **context and explanation, not forecasts**. Any macro
  "view" is a calibrated judgment about the *backdrop*, not a prediction of a price or
  exchange rate.
- The market prices public information in within seconds; a model that reads the news
  is structurally behind the market. Do not treat any output here as a trading signal.
- Calibration numbers (Brier / ECE) describe how well *past* judgments were calibrated;
  they are **not** indicative of future performance.
- No representation is made that any strategy, model or view will be profitable or
  suitable for any purpose.
